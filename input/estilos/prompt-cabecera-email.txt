# Cabeceras de correo y newsletter (v2, estilo Pains)

Para qué: la franja de imagen que abre un correo de marketing o una newsletter de Visual Trans.
Mismo lenguaje que los posts de Pains (foto real + UNA pieza de interfaz que hace de metáfora),
adaptado a una franja muy horizontal que se ve pequeña (600 px de ancho) y con el titular puesto
después en la herramienta de correo, a la izquierda.

Ajustes: generar en 21:9 (o 2:1) y recortar a 3:1 (1200×400). `channel: digital`. 1 imagen.
Al prompt de abajo se le añade la idea concreta del correo (producto, novedad, problema que
resuelve) y la metáfora de interfaz elegida para ella, en inglés.

Ejemplo de idea: correo de vExtract (la IA lee facturas y packing lists y monta las partidas del
DUA) → foto de un escritorio de agencia de aduanas con una pila de facturas en papel a la
derecha; encima, una tarjeta de cristal con barra de progreso "Leyendo factura… 12 partidas" y
un botón verde "Pasar al DUA".

--- PROMPT ---
STYLE PROMPT – Visual Trans email header

Concept: Create a wide email header image for the provided email topic. Translate its key idea into a realistic editorial photograph combined with ONE carefully crafted digital UI element that works as a clever visual metaphor or witty pun. The meaning must come from the interaction between the real scene and the interface: the UI should feel like it belongs to that world, not pasted on top.

Digital metaphor (the most important part):
- The UI element must be specific, believable and polished, like a real product interface: title bars, icons, labels, buttons, states (selected, loading, error, success), cursors or progress indicators when they add meaning.
- It should interact with the photo: point at, select, highlight, scan, measure, alert or "edit" something real in the scene (a crosshair marking a container, a scan line over a stack of invoices, a "System error" dialog above a stuck truck, a green "Done" check on a cleared shipment).
- Very short UI copy in Spanish with a touch of irony or humor: two to five words plus a label or number. It must stay legible when the image is shown at 600 px wide, so use few, large words.
- Consistent integration: UI shadows, perspective and scale coherent with the scene's lighting; subtle background blur behind glass panels.

Background: high-quality real photography of the logistics and customs world (container ports, freight trains, trucks on European motorways, warehouses, air cargo terminals, a real customs or freight-forwarding office with people working, not looking at the camera), or a full-bleed clean texture (vintage paper map, blurred world map, sea from above) for institutional emails.
Photo look: bright natural daylight, clear blue skies, clean and slightly desaturated colors, sharp focus, no dark or dramatic mood. Optional subtle film grain.

UI style:
- Glassmorphism: frosted translucent white panels, rounded corners (~20px), soft drop shadows.
- Formats: search bars, OS-style dialog windows, floating cards with icon tiles and data pills, progress bars, status labels, notifications.
- Icons: flat white line icons inside rounded-square solid tiles. Data in pill-shaped colored labels.
- Typography: clean geometric sans-serif (Inter-like) for messages; monospaced font (JetBrains Mono-like) for numbers, codes and labels.

Color palette: dominant sky/sea blues and whites. Accents used sparingly: cyan #00B4E6, orange #F39200, navy blue #2E5590, bright red #E30613, green #2EAA5C for positive data.

Composition: very wide banner. The main subject and the single UI element (or one cohesive group) sit together in the right half of the frame, vertically centered so nothing important is lost when cropped to 3:1. The left 40-45% is calm, uniform negative space (sky, sea, light wall or soft blur) for a headline added later. Minimal and uncluttered, one clear focal point.

Logo and text: NO logo or brand mark anywhere (leave the bottom-right corner free). No headline, no flags, no readable signage; the only text is the short UI copy.

Avoid: clutter, several unrelated overlays, long text, generic or decorative UI with no meaning, cartoon or 3D render style, stock clichés (handshakes, smiling suits, chess, light bulbs, puzzles, globes with network lines, arrows, holograms), dark lighting, heavy filters, neon.
